# ON AIR v2.6

Start with **bambu-studio/on-air-v26-X1C-eSUN-PLA-plus-fit-checks.3mf**. Its four objects are the upper housing, yoke, reset button and matching front-frame section. Use four M3×8 button-head screws and four nuts for this sample.

After testing, replace production parts **01, 05, 06 and 07** together. All eight production screws are now **M3×8 button heads**. Read REVISION-NOTES.md and BUILD-AND-ASSEMBLY.md before assembly.

Solder XIAO wires outside the case. Install reset first, then insert the XIAO 8 mm low and slide upward. Flip the charger so its LEDs face the rear holes. Check that MODE stays seated and reset returns freely.

First-layer inspection remains disabled. Keep the supplied print orientations. Acrylic SVGs, graphic backing and optical retainer remain compatible. The next physical sample is the acceptance check.
